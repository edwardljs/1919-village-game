# Third-party 3D assets

The following models are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

- **Yongjun** — KOREA HERITAGE SERVICE [KHS], [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/yongjun-1249db1e427e457f8c18ac73d8b1002a), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Gyeongbokgung Gangnyeongjeon Table A** — National Heritage Administration, [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gyeongbokgung-gangnyeongjeon-tablea-b16f47a09d0e4399af6274afceb4982f), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The models are redistributed in GLB form without mesh edits. Runtime transforms, scale, shadow settings, and placement are applied by the game.

The following lightweight environment assets are also used:

- **Nature Kit** — Kenney, [official asset page](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit), Creative Commons CC0. Selected GLBs: oak tree, pine tree, grass, stone, log stack, and yellow flower.
- **Stork.glb** — [three.js example asset](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Stork.glb), distributed with the [three.js repository](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) under the MIT License.
